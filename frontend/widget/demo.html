Demo host page

This page stands in for a product (the bookkeeping app, Momo, …). The chat launcher in the bottom-right corner is the Service Hub widget, embedded with a single script tag:

<script src="/widget.js" data-hub-key="REPLACE_WITH_TENANT_PUBLIC_KEY" async></script>

Seed a demo tenant first (python backend/scripts/seed_demo.py), paste its public key into the script tag below in this file, reload, and chat. Try asking about opening hours — or say the word chargeback to watch an escalation land in the operator console at /console.
